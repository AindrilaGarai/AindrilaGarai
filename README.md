# Aindrila Garai
### Statistics × Machine Learning × Adaptive Learning

> Developing statistical methods that learn, adapt, and remain
> reliable as the world changes.

I am a PhD researcher working at the intersection of statistics
and machine learning. My research explores how predictive models
can adapt to evolving data environments, particularly when
structural changes make traditional forecasting methods less reliable.

### PhD Supervisor: [Dr Matteo Fasiolo](https://github.com/mfasiolo)

## Research Overview

My research focuses on developing adaptive forecasting methods using time-varying learning rates and forgetting factors. I explore two approaches: modelling forgetting factors as functions of covariates with nonlinear effects and automatic smoothness selection, and combining competing forgetting factors using online aggregation methods such as Exponentially Weighted Aggregation (EWA) and ordinal stacking. Another area of my research focuses on developing adaptive strategies for selecting the optimal number of iterations in machine learning algorithms, particularly in online learning and rolling-window settings where data and model performance evolve over time.
